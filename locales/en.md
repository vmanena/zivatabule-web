# ZiwaBoard. Practical tools for teachers and trainers.

**No account. No ads. No tracking. Free for everyone.**

Timers, polls, random picks, group assignments, QR codes and other tools you can use in class every day. All in one place, ready to use without signing up.

[Try ZiwaBoard](https://ziwaboard.com/)

**[Václav Maněna](https://manena.info) and a team of practising teachers develop and run ZiwaBoard.** We draw on our classroom experience to build tools we need and use ourselves.

## From primary school to workplace training

Teachers in **primary and secondary schools** use ZiwaBoard to involve students, organise activities and simplify everyday teaching.

Polls, timers, random picks, group assignments and QR codes for sharing links also come in handy for **trainers running workshops, courses and staff training.** Open your browser and you’re ready to go.
